Do
  • Open a section with a provocation: Trust in automation.
  • End the setup on a real question ending in "?"
  • Title Case headlines, sentence case everything else
  • Treat numerals as design elements — "2026", "00 — Intro"
  • German for course-specific copy, English for the template
Don't
  • Exclamation points
  • Emoji — anywhere
  • First-person marketing "we"
  • Stacked adjectives ("revolutionary next-gen")
  • Filler stats that aren't sourced
